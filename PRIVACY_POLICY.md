# Privacy Policy

**Moving & Storage Driver App**

**Last Updated: February 11, 2026**

**Effective Date: February 11, 2026**

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Moving & Storage Driver ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the Moving & Storage Driver mobile application (the "App"). This App is designed for truck drivers to manage moving and storage delivery orders, capture proof of delivery, and track order progress.

By downloading, installing, or using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide Directly

- **Account Information**: Email address and password used to log in to the App.
- **Driver Profile Information**: Name, phone number, email address, driver ID, license number, and truck ID.

### 2.2 Information Collected Through App Usage

- **Order and Delivery Data**: Customer names, phone numbers, email addresses, pickup and dropoff addresses (including city, state, and zip code), scheduled and actual pickup/delivery dates, order status updates, and special instructions or notes.
- **Inventory Information**: Item descriptions, quantities, weights, fragility indicators, and special handling notes associated with delivery orders.
- **Proof of Delivery**: Photos captured using the device camera, delivery timestamps, delivery notes, and driver signatures.

### 2.3 Device Permissions and Data

- **Camera**: Used to capture proof of delivery photos. Photos are stored locally on your device.
- **Photo Library**: Used to allow selection of existing photos for delivery documentation.
- **Location**: Used to optimize delivery routes and track order progress. Location data may include GPS coordinates associated with pickup and dropoff locations.
- **Storage**: Used to read and write delivery photos and app data on your device.

### 2.4 Automatically Collected Information

- **Usage Logs**: The App may log operational events such as authentication events, order status changes, and navigation events. In the current version, these logs are stored locally on the device and are not transmitted to external servers.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

- **Providing Core Functionality**: To enable order management, status tracking, route planning, and proof of delivery capture.
- **Authentication**: To verify your identity and maintain your session within the App.
- **Order Management**: To display, update, and manage delivery orders and associated customer information.
- **Delivery Documentation**: To capture and store proof of delivery photos and related records.
- **App Improvement**: To monitor usage patterns and improve the reliability, performance, and functionality of the App.

## 4. Data Storage and Security

### 4.1 Local Storage

The App stores data locally on your device using secure on-device storage mechanisms. This includes your session information, order data, and delivery photos. Data stored locally is subject to the security protections provided by your device's operating system.

### 4.2 Data Security Measures

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include:

- Secure on-device storage for session and order data.
- No transmission of personal data over the internet without appropriate security measures.
- Session management with the ability to log out and clear session data.

### 4.3 Data Retention

- **Session Data**: Retained on your device until you log out, at which point it is cleared.
- **Order Data**: Retained on your device for the duration necessary to fulfill delivery operations.
- **Photos**: Stored locally on your device in the App's document directory and retained until manually deleted.

## 5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information in the following limited circumstances:

- **Service Providers**: We may share data with trusted service providers who assist in operating the App or conducting our business, subject to confidentiality obligations.
- **Legal Requirements**: We may disclose information if required to do so by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities (e.g., a court order or government agency).
- **Business Transfers**: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you before your information becomes subject to a different privacy policy.
- **With Your Consent**: We may share your information for any other purpose with your explicit consent.

## 6. Third-Party Services

The App is built using the Expo framework and may use the following third-party components:

- **Expo SDK**: Provides core app functionality including camera access, image picking, and device information. Expo's privacy policy can be found at [https://expo.dev/privacy](https://expo.dev/privacy).
- **Expo Application Services (EAS)**: Used for building and distributing the App. EAS may collect build-related metadata.

The App does not currently integrate third-party analytics, advertising, or crash reporting services. Should we integrate such services in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

## 7. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of consent in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

## 8. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights regarding your personal information:

- **Access**: You may request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
- **Correction**: You may request that we correct inaccurate or incomplete personal information.
- **Deletion**: You may request that we delete your personal information. Note that uninstalling the App removes all locally stored data from your device.
- **Restrict Processing**: You may request that we restrict the processing of your personal information.
- **Data Portability**: You may request that we provide your data in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format.
- **Withdraw Consent**: You may withdraw consent for data processing at any time by adjusting your device permissions or uninstalling the App.

### Managing Device Permissions

You can control the App's access to your device features at any time through your device settings:

- **Camera**: Settings > Apps > Moving & Storage Driver > Permissions > Camera
- **Location**: Settings > Apps > Moving & Storage Driver > Permissions > Location
- **Storage/Photos**: Settings > Apps > Moving & Storage Driver > Permissions > Storage / Photos

Disabling certain permissions may limit the functionality of the App.
